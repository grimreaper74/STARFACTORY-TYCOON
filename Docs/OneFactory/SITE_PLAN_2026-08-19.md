data). North = +Y world.

## Honest current state

The playable map contains NO site dressing: an actor sweep found zero
fence, road, lorry, container, tower or landscaping actors - every
"gate/truck" match was an interior press actor. The earlier site work
(old Task 29) predates the separate-buildings rebuild and its content is
not in this map. The four shop shells stand on a bare apron.

## Designed site (to build as saved actors)

- **Perimeter fence** 740 x 400 m centred (0, 500): SM_Fence_01 /
  SM_FencePart_01 family, with the owned gate leaves at two entries.
- **Ring road** 8 m tarmac inside the fence; **spine road** east-west
  through the central yard; north straight is the HGV route.
- **Main gate + gatehouse** at the NE corner (gatehouse ~ (34200,
  13000)); **west service gate** mid west fence.
- **Coil intake yard** west of press (x ~ -32200): HGV docks, approved
  inbound lorry, adjustable coil stands (rename their MeshyPBR materials
  before shipping placement - documented U-6 debt), container rows.
- **Dispatch compound** north of assembly's shutter (y ~ 16600):
  marshalling lanes for finished Cairnwells, guard fence, exit to the
  main gate; solar canopy over the lanes (2040 flavour, kit-buildable).
- **Chemical delivery dock** south of paint (mix room/ED chemistry);
  **container/trailer yard** south of body.
- **Substation + battery farm** east of paint outside the ring road -
  an EV plant that stores its own energy (authored kit piece later).
- **Skyline**: Background2 towers/hangar OUTSIDE the fence to the NW.
- **Landscaping**: grass strips inside the fence are material-only;
  trees remain the known vegetation gap (owner's Fab account or
  Blender-authored batch).

Lights-out consequence: no staff car park - a small visitor/maintenance
stand at the gatehouse only. AGV routes matter more than footpaths.

## Build plan

Batches of saved actors with the usual gates: (1) fence + gates +
gatehouse, (2) roads + yard slabs + markings, (3) coil yard + dispatch
compound dressing, (4) skyline + substation + canopy. Owned Fab packs
carry most of it (fence family, gate leaves, containers, modular truck,
background towers/hangar); the approved lorry/coil stand need their
material rename first.

## Dispatch scale (owner, 2026-08-19)

Real plants hold huge finished-vehicle yards; the owner's call is not to
show that. The dispatch compound stays small and legible: two or three
marshalling lanes (10-20 visible cars) that fill as contract batches
complete and empty out the main gate, with staged car-transporter
lorries implying the wider outbound chain. Show the last twenty cars,
imply the rest.
