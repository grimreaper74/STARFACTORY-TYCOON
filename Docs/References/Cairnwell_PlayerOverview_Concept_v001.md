## The target look

- A clean, bright, high-detail automotive factory, seen from a moderately elevated player overview.
- Light neutral concrete/tiled floor with broad, immediately readable green route lanes bounded by restrained yellow safety lines.
- Dense but orderly factory equipment: credible frames, guards, rollers, service cabinets, work envelopes, cable runs and material-handling details. No large featureless grey shells.
- Primary machine bodies are warm white/pale grey and graphite, with controlled Cairnwell-green fields. Yellow is a hazard/guard/route colour, not a general accent.
- Open process areas reveal believable mechanisms; skins/enclosures add equipment density without hiding required process identity.
- Walls are dark charcoal industrial bays with warm glazing; the space is brightly and evenly lit enough to read station condition from the overview camera.
